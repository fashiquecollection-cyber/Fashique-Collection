## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory, bespoke world of handcrafted botanical resin art and luxury artisanal jewellery. The visual language balances editorial refinement with an intimate atelier spirit. It evokes an emotional response of curated exclusivity, organic grace, and tactile appreciation. 

The aesthetic is Modern Editorial Luxury infused with Soft Tactile Glassmorphism. Space is treated like gallery walls or heavy archival paper: deliberate, serene, and unhurried. Generous negative space frames high-resolution close-ups of suspended flora, gold leaf inclusions, and hand-polished resin contours. Micro-interactions are deliberate and liquid, featuring slow-dissolve opacities and hairline gold luminescence on hover.

## Colors

The palette mirrors organic materials: raw cream paper, aged metals, preserved petals, and deep organic carbon.

- **Primary (`#221C1B` - Espresso Charcoal):** Grounded, high-contrast, and softer than pure black. Used for high-impact typography, primary actionable buttons, and definitive structural anchors.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Antique Champagne Gold):** A satin, muted metallic used for subtle badges, secondary CTAs, focus indicators, interactive hover glimmers, and refined ornamental hairline rules.
- **Tertiary (`#E8B4B8` - Soft Blush Rose):** Used selectively for delicate alerts, bespoke gifting tags, romantic highlights, and seasonal curation badges.
- **Neutral (`#F9F5EE` - Atelier Cream):** The warm foundation. Canvas layers transition between pure ivory (`#FDFBF7`) for elevated containers, standard cream (`#F9F5EE`) for body backgrounds, and warm biscuit (`#EADDC9`) for dividers and borders.

Light mode serves as the exclusive default experience to preserve the luminous, airy transparency essential to botanical resin aesthetics.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy creates an haute-couture editorial balance.

- **Headlines:** Set in `Playfair Display`. The high-contrast serif structure brings literary craftsmanship, romantic flare, and sophistication to collection titles, storytelling narrative banners, and hero statements.
- **Body & Controls:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its geometric clarity ensures exceptional legibility for dense product specs, artisanal materials lists, provenance notes, and checkout interactions.
- **Meta & Badging:** The `label-uppercase` style requires explicit transformation to uppercase paired with `+0.15em` tracking. This treatment is strictly applied to category kickers, price prefixes, curated edition tags, and tab navigation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial grid designed for visual rhythm, asymmetrical object balance, and ample breathing room.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile (320px–767px): 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
  - Tablet (768px–1023px): 8 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
  - Desktop (1024px+): 12 columns, `margin: 5rem`, `gutter: 2.5rem`, capped at a max-width container of `1440px`.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Generous section spacing (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) separates curated stories, allowing individual bespoke creations to exist without visual crowding.
- **Asymmetric Composition:** Product features frequently stagger between 7-column editorial spreads (narrative, resin process video, floral extraction notes) and 5-column product display cards to break rigid e-commerce modularity.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy synthetic shadows. Instead, it simulates the translucent, layered depth of light passing through liquid resin and polished gemstones.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base Canvas: Solid `#F9F5EE` (Atelier Cream).
  - Floating Canvas / Cards: `#FDFBF7` (Ivory).
  - Glass Overlays & Floating Navigation: Background `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.72)` combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(140%)` and a hairline border of `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.
- **Ambient Shadow System:**
  - Soft Rest: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(34, 28, 27, 0.03), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(34, 28, 27, 0.02)`.
  - Hover / Elevate: `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(34, 28, 27, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`. The secondary shadow incorporates a warm gold undertone that mimics backlighting through amber and clear resin.
- **Dividers & Structural Borders:** 1px hairline rules using `#EADDC9`. Dividers between high-prestige sections may taper with a radial opacity gradient (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` to transparent).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts `Soft` geometry (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Base micro-elements (buttons, inputs, select triggers) feature subtle `0.25rem` (4px) rounded corners, providing an architectural, tailored finish rather than an overly digital feel.
- Medium components (cards, dialogs, drawers) use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Modals, large feature media windows, and botanical showcase containers use `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- Badges and pill tags represent the sole exception: fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to contrast against linear editorial typography.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Solid `#221C1B` background, `#FDFBF7` label text, `0.25rem` radius, 48px height, `label-uppercase` typography. Hover transforms background to `#38302E` with a smooth 200ms ease and brings a subtle `0 0 12px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)` glow.
  - *Secondary / Atelier:* Transparent background, `1px solid #C5A059`, `#221C1B` text. Hover transitions to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` fill with `#B89047` border.
  - *Tertiary / Text Link:* Underlined by an offset 1px antique gold border that expands from the center on hover.
- **Chips & Tags:**
  - Compact pill forms (`rounded-full`) padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
  - Background `rgba(232, 180, 184, 0.15)` with `#38302E` text for botanical variants; background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)` with `#7C6120` text for limited/customized editions.
- **Input Fields:**
  - Background `#FDFBF7`, bordered with `1px solid #EADDC9`, corner radius `0.25rem`. Height 48px.
  - Floating labels in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Focused state transitions border to `#C5A059` with a subtle ring of `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)` (2px spread), no harsh browser blue outlines.
- **Checkboxes & Radio Buttons:**
  - Custom 18px squares/circles in `#FDFBF7` with `1px solid #C5A059`. 
  - Checked state fills with `#221C1B` displaying an antique gold (`#D4AF37`) hairline checkmark or concentric inner dot.
- **Cards (Product & Botanical Showcase):**
  - Background `#FDFBF7`, framed with `1px solid rgba(234, 221, 201, 0.6)`. 
  - Image containers utilize an aspect ratio of 4:5 or 1:1 with an ultra-soft beige underlay (`#FAF7F2`) to accommodate transparent resin renders.
  - Hover triggers an image micro-scale (1.025x) over 600ms cubic-bezier transition alongside a border shift to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)`.
- **Atelier Customizer / Gifting Drawer:**
  - A slide-in sheet featuring frosted glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88)`). 
  - Segmented controls allow real-time selection of resin tints, botanical species (e.g., Pressed Hydrangea, Gold Leaf Flakes), and customized engraving typography previews.
